I'm a goalkeeper, coach, and founder working where sports and business meet. I played three years as an NCAA Division I goalkeeper at the University of South Florida in the American Athletic Conference, and I now play NAIA soccer at Indiana Wesleyan University. I also compete at the semi-pro level in USL League Two with FC Miami City, Baller League USA, and the UPSL.

Off the field, I served as Head Goalkeeper Coach at FC Prime in Miami and run my own private goalkeeper training business. I founded Ralph Montero LLC, which houses my coaching business and Verlantic AI, my AI venture.

Today I'm pursuing my master's degree in Artificial Intelligence at Indiana Wesleyan University. My goal is to help athletes, coaches, and small sports businesses use AI to save time, serve their people better, and make smarter decisions. This portfolio documents that journey, one project at a time.
